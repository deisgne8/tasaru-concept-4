The supplier platform

Developed by TASARU Mobility Investments, TSH connects global automotive knowledge with manufacturing capability in Saudi Arabia. Its role centers on land, real estate and equipment for the five-supplier platform.

Five suppliers at the heart of the hub

Lear, Shin Young, BENTELER, Fangxin and JVIS are central to the TSH story. Together, the supplier network gives the platform a clear focus on specialist automotive manufacturing expertise.

Critical capabilities closer to production

The hub’s story spans seating, body-in-white, chassis, interior plastics and exterior plastics. These capabilities connect the platform to essential parts of the automotive value chain and the Kingdom’s growing OEM base.
